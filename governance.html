Governance Framework

The governance framework defines the policies, responsibilities, processes and controls used to manage telecom data throughout the organization.

Active

Governance Objectives

  • Ensure data accuracy and consistency
  • Protect sensitive telecom information
  • Define data ownership and responsibility
  • Improve regulatory compliance
  • Support secure data usage

Data Ownership

Data owners are responsible for defining data requirements, access permissions, quality standards and appropriate usage of organizational data.

Data Stewardship

Data stewards monitor data quality, maintain governance policies and coordinate data-related activities between business and technical teams.
