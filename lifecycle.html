Telecom Data Retention, Archiving and Secure Disposal

← Back to Dashboard

1. Data Creation

Telecom customer and operational data is generated and collected.

2. Data Storage

Data is stored in controlled systems with appropriate access restrictions.

3. Data Usage

Authorized teams use data for approved business and operational purposes.

4. Data Archiving

Inactive data is moved to secure archive storage according to retention requirements.

5. Data Review

Stored and archived data is periodically reviewed for retention and business requirements.

6. Secure Disposal

Data that has reached the end of its retention period is securely deleted.

Data Retention Policy

Data Category Retention Approach Status
Customer Records Retain according to approved business and regulatory requirements. Managed
Billing Information Retain for the defined financial and operational period. Managed
Audit Logs Retain according to security and audit requirements. Managed
Archived Data Review periodically and dispose when retention requirements expire. Managed

Backup & Archiving

  • Regular backups of important telecom information
  • Secure archive storage for inactive records
  • Backup integrity verification
  • Controlled access to archived information
  • Periodic recovery testing

Secure Disposal

  • Identify records that have reached the end of their retention period.
  • Obtain appropriate authorization for disposal.
  • Securely delete digital records.
  • Maintain disposal records and audit evidence.
  • Periodically review the disposal process.
